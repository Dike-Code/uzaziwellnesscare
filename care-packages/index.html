How To Choose

Which package is right for me?

The right package depends on your stage, your support system, and what feels manageable. Here are some starting points.

Choose Essential if…

You have nearby family or partner support and want professional guidance to layer on top — especially in the early weeks after birth.

Choose Signature if…

You want full fourth-trimester support — birth prep, postpartum care, feeding help, mental health check-ins, and nutrition guidance.

Choose Premium if…

You want comprehensive care from pregnancy through full recovery — with priority scheduling and unlimited access to your care team.

Still not sure? Book a free consultation — we'll help you decide.
