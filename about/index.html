Our Story

Care that meets mothers where they are

Uzazi Wellness Care exists for one reason: motherhood deserves better support than it usually gets.

Why Uzazi

Too often, mothers are left to figure things out alone. Pregnancy advice scattered across the internet. Postpartum recovery framed as something to "get through." Mental health treated as an afterthought. Feeding guidance laced with shame.

We started Uzazi Wellness Care because we believe every mother deserves gentle, professional, judgment-free support — and that support should come to her, not the other way around.

Our name, Uzazi, simply means motherhood. It's a word that carries weight, history, and tenderness — and we hold all of that in the care we offer.

What We Believe

The principles that guide our care

Mother-centered

Every plan starts with the mother — her story, her needs, her pace. Nothing is one-size-fits-all.

Judgment-free

However you feed, however you birth, however you parent — we support you without shame or pressure.

Trained & thoughtful

Our care specialists are trained, prepared, and continuously learning — because mothers deserve real expertise.

In-home, every time

We come to you. Every visit. Because care should fit your life, not interrupt it.

Continuity matters

The same care team across visits. Trust deepens, care gets better, and you don't have to re-tell your story.

Family-inclusive

We support mothers — and we welcome partners, families, and the people who love them into the work.

Our Care Network

A trusted circle of specialists, working alongside us

We've cultivated relationships with vetted, experienced practitioners across maternal health and wellness. When your needs go beyond what our postpartum care specialists provide, we coordinate care with these trusted partners — so you never have to start from scratch finding the right person.

Lactation

Lactation Specialists & IBCLCs

Internationally Board Certified Lactation Consultants for breastfeeding challenges, supply concerns, and feeding plans tailored to your family.

Bodywork

Postpartum Massage Therapists

Licensed therapists trained in prenatal and postpartum bodywork — gentle techniques to ease tension, support recovery, and honor a body that has just done extraordinary work.

Movement

Postnatal Fitness Coaches

Certified personal trainers specializing in safe return-to-movement programs after birth — diastasis-aware, pelvic-floor conscious, and built around your timeline.

Nutrition

Nutritionists & Dietitians

Registered nutrition professionals who specialize in pregnancy, postpartum, and breastfeeding nutrition — for energy, healing, and long-term wellness.

Mental Health

Postpartum Therapists & Counselors

Licensed mental health professionals trained in perinatal mood and anxiety disorders — for the moments when emotional support needs to come from a clinical expert.

Pelvic Health

Pelvic Floor Physical Therapists

Specialized PTs who address the often-overlooked recovery work most mothers need — leakage, prolapse, painful intimacy, core healing, and beyond.

Birth

Doulas & Birth Doulas

Trained labor companions who provide continuous emotional and physical support during pregnancy, birth, and the early postpartum window.

Sleep

Infant Sleep Consultants

Gentle, evidence-based sleep specialists who help families build healthy rest patterns without rigid schedules — for both baby and exhausted parents.

Spinal Care

Prenatal & Postnatal Chiropractors

Webster-certified and perinatal-trained chiropractors who address the spinal and pelvic shifts of pregnancy and the demands of carrying and feeding a newborn.

Education

Childbirth Educators

Certified educators who guide expectant families through evidence-based birth preparation — your options, your body, your choices.

Physicians

OB/GYNs & Specialist Doctors

A trusted referral network of board-certified physicians — OB/GYNs for routine and complex pregnancy care, plus reproductive endocrinologists and endometriosis-trained specialists for fertility journeys, recurring pain, and hormonal conditions.

All partners are personally vetted by our team. Specific referrals are matched to your needs and shared during your consultation or care planning session.

What We're Not

Honest about our scope

We believe being clear about what we don't do is just as important as being clear about what we do.

We are not your medical provider

We do not replace your doctor, midwife, or licensed therapist. We work alongside your medical team — never instead of them.

We do not diagnose

If something requires clinical attention, we connect you with licensed providers and stay alongside you through the process.

Let's start with a conversation

30 minutes, no commitment. Tell us where you are, and we'll listen.
