Emotional Support

Mental Health

Confidential, compassionate emotional support during one of life's biggest transitions — from pregnancy through postpartum and beyond.

What we do

  • Confidential one-on-one emotional support sessions
  • In-home check-ins so you don't have to leave the house
  • Virtual support when in-home isn't possible
  • Help recognizing what you're feeling and naming it
  • Coping strategies and self-care planning
  • Referrals to licensed mental health professionals when needed

Who it's for

  • Mothers feeling overwhelmed, anxious, or low
  • Anyone struggling with the emotional weight of new motherhood
  • Families navigating loss, transition, or uncertainty
  • Women who want to build emotional resilience proactively
What to Expect

How a typical care plan unfolds

1

Confidential first conversation

No assessment, no diagnosis — just a real conversation about how you're doing.

2

Personalized support plan

We design a plan that fits your comfort level — in-home visits, virtual support, or a mix.

3

Ongoing check-ins

Regular sessions help you feel heard, build coping tools, and notice your own progress.

4

Referrals when needed

If you need clinical mental health care, we help you connect with licensed professionals — and stay alongside you through it.

Working with our care network: Postpartum Therapists & Counselors

Mental health support spans a spectrum. Our specialists provide compassionate listening and practical coping support, but when you need clinical care, we coordinate seamlessly with licensed postpartum therapists and counselors in our care network. Meet the network →

Common Questions

Frequently asked

Are you licensed therapists?

No — we provide trained emotional support, not clinical therapy. When clinical care is needed, we refer you to licensed mental health professionals and continue alongside you.

Is this confidential?

Yes. Conversations stay between you and your care specialist. We share nothing without your permission.

Can I do this virtually?

Yes. While we prefer in-home support, we offer virtual sessions when that's easier for you.

Begin with a free consultation

30 minutes, no commitment. We'll listen and recommend the right next step for you.
