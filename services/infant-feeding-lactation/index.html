Feeding Support

Infant Feeding & Lactation

Hands-on, judgment-free feeding support — for whatever path works for your family. Breastfeeding, bottle, combo, or transitioning — we meet you there.

What we do

  • In-home breastfeeding support and latch guidance
  • Pumping and milk supply troubleshooting
  • Bottle and combo feeding setup
  • Help transitioning between feeding methods
  • Solid food introduction guidance (around 6 months)
  • Practical, family-centered education — no shame, no pressure

Who it's for

  • New mothers needing feeding support in the early weeks
  • Mothers returning to work and starting to pump
  • Families navigating combo feeding or formula
  • Babies needing extra patience and a different approach
What to Expect

How a typical care plan unfolds

1

Feeding consultation

We talk through what's working, what isn't, and what kind of feeding journey feels right for your family.

2

In-home feeding sessions

Hands-on guidance during real feeds. We adjust positioning, timing, and tools as needed.

3

Continuous adjustment

Feeding evolves. We stay flexible — supporting you whether your path changes at week 2 or week 12.

4

Confident handoff

When you feel steady, we step back. We're always available to come back if something shifts.

Working with our care network: Lactation Specialists & IBCLCs

When breastfeeding challenges need a deeper level of clinical expertise — supply concerns, tongue-tie evaluation, complex latch issues — we coordinate directly with our partner Internationally Board Certified Lactation Consultants. You stay supported by us; they bring the specialized clinical lens. Meet the network →

Common Questions

Frequently asked

I want to formula feed — will you still help?

Yes. There is no judgment here. We support whatever feeding path your family chooses, and we help you do it well.

What if I'm struggling with latch?

This is one of our most common starting points. Many feeding challenges resolve with patient, in-person guidance — and we'll work through it with you.

Do you help with pumping for working mothers?

Absolutely. We help you build a pumping rhythm, choose the right setup, and prepare for the return to work.
