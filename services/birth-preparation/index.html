Birth Prep

Birth Preparation

Childbirth education, calm coping strategies, and partner preparation — so you walk into birth feeling informed, supported, and ready.

What we do

  • Comprehensive childbirth education in your home
  • Breathing, movement, and comfort techniques
  • Birth plan creation and rehearsal
  • Partner coaching and support skills
  • What to pack, what to expect, what to ask
  • Postpartum readiness conversations

Who it's for

  • Expectant mothers in the third trimester
  • Couples who want to prepare together at their own pace
  • Anyone wanting an alternative to large group classes
  • Mothers preparing for VBAC or a different birth experience
What to Expect

How a typical care plan unfolds

1

Birth Prep consultation

We talk about your birth hopes, concerns, and any prior experiences. We design a course that fits your timeline.

2

In-home class series

Education unfolds across several visits — covering stages of labor, comfort techniques, and partner roles.

3

Practice and rehearsal

We rehearse positions, breathing patterns, and the practical mechanics of getting through labor calmly.

4

Birth plan finalized

You leave with a clear plan, confidence in your team, and a partner who knows exactly how to support you.

Working with our care network: Doulas & Childbirth Educators

Birth is a deeply personal milestone. We collaborate with experienced doulas and certified childbirth educators in our care network — so the team supporting you through pregnancy can extend right into your birth experience. Meet the network →

Common Questions

Frequently asked

How many sessions are included?

Most birth prep care plans run between 4 and 8 sessions, depending on your needs and timeline. We design it together at your free consultation.

Do you cover both vaginal and cesarean preparation?

Yes. We prepare you for the most likely path while making sure you understand all possibilities — including planned and unplanned cesarean.

Can my partner join all sessions?

Absolutely. Partner participation is encouraged and built into our approach.
