Postnatal Care

Postnatal Recovery

Gentle, professional in-home support during the fourth trimester — so you can rest, heal, and get to know your baby with someone trained alongside you.

What we do

  • In-home postpartum visits during the first 12 weeks
  • Recovery guidance after vaginal or cesarean birth
  • Newborn care education and reassurance
  • Sleep, feeding, and rhythm support
  • Help with the day-to-day so you can rest
  • Emotional check-ins as you transition

Who it's for

  • New mothers in the first weeks after birth
  • Mothers recovering from cesarean or complicated birth
  • Families without nearby support who need extra help
  • Anyone who wants a calmer, more rested fourth trimester
What to Expect

How a typical care plan unfolds

1

Welcome home visit

Within days of bringing baby home, your care specialist arrives to check in, reassure, and start care.

2

Regular in-home visits

Visits unfold across the first weeks, focused on rest, feeding, recovery, and newborn care.

3

Education layered in gently

You learn at your pace — never overwhelmed, never lectured. Practical, hands-on, judgment-free.

4

Smooth handoff

As you find your rhythm, we taper visits and transition you to community support and follow-up resources.

Working with our care network: Massage Therapists, Pelvic Floor PTs & Postnatal Fitness Coaches

Recovery is a body-wide journey. We coordinate with licensed postpartum massage therapists, pelvic floor physical therapists, and postnatal fitness coaches in our care network — so you get the right hands and the right movement at the right stage of healing. Meet the network →

Common Questions

Frequently asked

When does postnatal care start?

Most families begin within the first week after birth — sometimes even within 24-48 hours of coming home. The earlier we start, the more rest you get.

How long does postnatal care last?

Care plans range from a few weeks to the full fourth trimester (12 weeks). We tailor based on your needs.

What if I had a cesarean?

Cesarean recovery is a major focus area for us. We bring extra attention to your physical recovery, mobility, and rest.
