A Different Kind of Care

Care that comes to your door

Every Uzazi Wellness Care service is delivered in your home. A trained postpartum care specialist comes to you — every visit takes place in the comfort of your own home.

This means more privacy, more rest, and more time with your baby. We bring the supplies. We adjust to your schedule. We meet you where you are.

  • One-on-one care, never rushed
  • Same care team across visits
  • Flexible appointment windows
  • No waiting rooms, no clinics
