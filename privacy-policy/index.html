Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 2026

Uzazi Wellness Care ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you share with us through this website and our care services.

What we collect

When you contact us through the website or sign up for a consultation, we may collect: your name, email address, phone number, where you are in your motherhood journey, and any details you choose to share about your needs.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule a consultation
  • To design a care plan that fits your needs
  • To send updates if you've subscribed to Mama's Newsletter
  • To improve our services and communication

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information except as needed to deliver care to you, and only with your knowledge.

Confidentiality of care

Conversations with your care specialist remain confidential. We share clinical or health-related details only with your explicit permission, and only with providers you authorize.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or request that we delete it. Reach us at info@uzaziwellnesscare.com.

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services evolve. The effective date at the top reflects the most recent version.

Contact

Questions? Reach out at info@uzaziwellnesscare.com.
